Logistics · Operations

Cambodia Freight Performance

Shipments, service and cost across 4 warehouses and 7 carriers, Jan–Aug 2026.

4,200shipments
85.3%on-time delivery
2.15avg transit days
$417Kfreight spend

What I built

  • Shipments by month, stacked by transport mode (air, rail, road, sea).
  • On-time delivery trend against a 92% target line.
  • Carrier scorecard: shipments, on-time %, transit days and cost per kg.
  • A "why shipments run late" breakdown by delay reason.
  • Slicers for warehouse, mode and carrier; a botanical theme to match the brand.

What it shows: on-time delivery stayed under the 92% target all period and dipped to about 81% in June–July. Weather is the top cause of delays, and two carriers sit below 80% on-time.

Retail · Food & beverage

Buttercup Coffee Sales Overview

Sales across 3 café stores, Jan–Jun 2026.

$403Krevenue
77Ktransactions
$5.20average ticket
113Kitems sold

What I built

  • Average daily revenue by week, one line per store.
  • A weekly rhythm heatmap of average transactions by hour and weekday.
  • Revenue by category and a top-products table.
  • Store and month slicers; a soft butter-and-blue theme.

What it shows: all three stores grew over the six months, with Riverside earning the most per day. The busiest time is 7–9 am, peaking on Saturday mornings, and the latte is the best-selling product.

Insurance · Executive reporting

Sales & Business Performance

A five-page insurance report where every page answers one question for management.

The five pages

  • Executive Overview: How is the business performing?
  • Sales Performance: Where is the result coming from?
  • Profitability: Is our growth producing profit?
  • Renewal & Customer Risk: What might we lose?
  • Customer & Policy Detail: Find the exact policy.

What I built

  • Premium against plan and last year, achievement % by channel, and premium by product line.
  • A drill-down table from channel to manager, salesperson, customer and policy.
  • A profit bridge (waterfall) from last year's net profit to this year's.
  • A dynamic warning banner that spots when premium grows but profit falls, and names the biggest cost increase.
  • Six shared slicers across all pages.
